About me

The engineer who reads the brief twice and asks why before writing the first line.

I build web applications for businesses and organizations using Laravel and Drupal, and I care about how they hold up long after day one.

My work covers the full stack: backend architecture, API integrations, custom Drupal modules, database design, and front-end implementation. I'm currently a Software Engineer at The ArkDev, where I work on production platforms for real clients with real deadlines.
